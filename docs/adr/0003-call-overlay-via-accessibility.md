# Call Overlay drives WhatsApp through an accessibility service

The Owner cannot find WhatsApp's flip-camera and hang-up buttons, which hide after a few seconds, and Android offers no way to restyle another app or call its in-call actions. We show our own large floating buttons over WhatsApp video calls and use an accessibility service to reveal and press WhatsApp's real buttons. We accept that this breaks when WhatsApp changes its call screen and must be maintained, and that a sideloaded app needs "Allow restricted settings" granted by hand before the service can be enabled.
